On this site, a heart ( {{< heart title="Favorite" width="1.3rem" height="1.3rem" >}} ) indicates one of my personal favorites. This is true across all content types.

---

I also have a rating scale for books. It's a 7-point ordinal scale, where 3 is the best and -3 is the worst. It's mostly just to help me remember what I've enjoyed and to give me a consistent, clear scale to reference.
